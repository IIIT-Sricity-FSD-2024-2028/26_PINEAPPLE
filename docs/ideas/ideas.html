3. Critical Deep Dives & Remediation Blueprint

#1. Lock Down Public & Unscoped User Endpoints (SEC-05, SEC-06, SEC-07)
CRITICAL SECURITY

Vulnerability: While /auth/signup & /auth/login enforce security controls, legacy endpoints on UsersController (@Public() POST /users, @Public() POST /users/login, GET /users, PATCH /users/:id, DELETE /users/:id) permit anonymous Super User creation, passwordless email logins, mass-assignment role/XP elevation, cross-user PII harvesting, and cross-account deletions.

Solution: Require @Roles('superuser') on POST /users and DELETE /users/:id; lock GET /users to Admin/SuperUser and GET /users/:id to self/admin; enforce allow-listed SelfUpdateUserDto (resetting emailVerified = false upon email modification); enforce non-empty password checks and lockouts on every login path.

#2. Resolve Dual Reputation Ledger Split-Brain (DAT-01, GAM-01)
CRITICAL INTEGRITY

Root Cause: GamificationRepository serves Leaderboard/Profile stats, while ReputationService maintains a separate entries[] array for Moderation & /users/me/reputation. On boot, ReputationService.backfillStartupSeedUsers() pushes seed values into GamificationRepository a second time, inflating Arjun Sharma from 87 to 174 Rep. Meanwhile, signups (+100), approved tasks (+8), and completion badges (+15) skip ReputationService.

Solution: Consolidate into a single unified ledger repository with idempotent startup seeding, bidirectional synchronization, and a 5-day minimum project age on completion badges.

#3. Complete Snapshot Durability for Financial & Config Stores (DUR-01)
CRITICAL DURABILITY

Root Cause: PersistenceService saves sessions (including escrowId) in mentorMarketplace, but omits EscrowService, PlatformConfigService, PortalAdminsService, MentorApplicationsService, DemoUpiProvider, and JobRunsService. Reloading a snapshot leaves funded sessions orphaned from escrow records, crashing releases and refunds.

Solution: Add exportState()/importState() across all 6 services and persist them atomically in PersistenceService.

#4. Cron Draft/Deleted Filtering, Account Deletion Cleanup & 100% <SafeLink> Parity (PRJ-07, PRJ-08, SEC-02, SEC-04)
HIGH PRIORITY

Problem: Cron runners close Draft projects and deduct missed-deadline penalties on deletedAt projects; collaborator account deletion keeps seats occupied in collaborators[], leaves open tasks assigned to "Deleted user", and fails to delete secure-cvs/ files; 6 frontend links (ProjectCard, AdminMentorApps, Workspaces) still bypass <SafeLink>.

Solution: Exclude Draft and deletedAt projects from cron penalties/closures, release member tasks & unlink CVs on account deletion, count deleted projects in 24h spam windows, and migrate the final 6 raw links to <SafeLink>.

#5. High-Value New Features: Admin Support Inbox, Templates & Mentor Calendar (ADM-03, PRJ-09, MEN-07)
NEW INNOVATIONS

Roadmap Upgrades: Build AdminSupport.jsx so admins can manage Phase 12 Project Closure Request cases and Help inquiries; offer category starter-task templates on CreateProject + dependency blockers; provide weekly UTC mentor availability slots with automated overlap prevention (MEN-06/MEN-07); and deploy Job 19 storageHygieneJob.
