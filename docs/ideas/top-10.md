# Top 10 Post-Phase 18 Action Items & High-Impact Ideas

All 10 original top proposals from the Phase 5 audit (`SEC-01`, `JOB-01`, `FIN-01`, `MOD-01`/`02`, `PRJ-01`, `PRJ-02`, `JOB-02`, `MEN-01`, `TSK-01`, `COL-01`) are **DONE** across Phases 6–18 (with only 6 remaining raw `<a href>` instances in `SEC-02`/`SEC-04`).  

This updated **Top 10** lists the highest-priority remaining security/durability fixes and brand-new high-value product ideas identified in the November 11, 2026 audit.

1. **Auth & User Controller Lockdown (`SEC-05`, `SEC-06`, `SEC-07`, `SEC-12`)** — Lock down `@Public() POST /users`, require passwords on `/users/login`, block `PATCH /users/:id` mass assignment (`role`/`xp`/`emailVerified`), guard `DELETE /users/:id`, restrict `GET /users/:id` PII leaks, and rate-limit `POST /admin/login`.
2. **Canonical Reputation Ledger Unification (`DAT-01`, `GAM-01`)** — Fix `ReputationService.backfillStartupSeedUsers()` doubling seeded users' rep (`87 -> 174`), unify `ReputationService` & `GamificationRepository` into ONE source of truth, and enforce minimum project age before awarding completion badges.
3. **Complete JSON Snapshot Parity Across Every Store (`DUR-01`)** — Add `EscrowService`, `PlatformConfigService`, `PortalAdminsService`, `MentorApplicationsService`, `DemoUpiProvider`, and `JobRunsService` to `PersistenceService` so escrow payouts & settings never break after a restart.
4. **Notification & Task Cross-Project Ownership Guards (`SEC-08`, `SEC-09`)** — Enforce `isProjectOwner` on `POST/DELETE /tasks`, `isProjectMember` + `ContentFilter` on task comments, user ownership on `GET/PUT/DELETE /notifications/:id`, and admin-only restriction on `POST /notifications`.
5. **Cron Job State Guards (`PRJ-07`)** — Exclude `Draft` and `deletedAt` projects from `closeEmptyProjects`, `applyDeadlinePenalties`, and `reviewDeadlineJob`; include deleted projects in the 24h daily spam limit; close pending requests on `softDeleteProject`.
6. **Upload Sanitization, Legacy Controller Cleanup & Complete `<SafeLink>` Rollout (`SEC-10`, `SEC-11`, `SEC-02`, `SEC-04`)** — Ban `image/svg+xml` uploads with magic-byte verification, require `'mentor_apps'` for admin CV access, close legacy `/governance` & `/mentorship` policy bypasses, and wrap the remaining 6 raw `<a href>` links in `<SafeLink>`.
7. **Account Deletion Lifecycle Parity & Mentor Schedule Safeguards (`PRJ-08`, `MEN-06`)** — Release Open/In-Progress tasks & vacate team capacity slots on collaborator account deletion, wipe uploaded CVs on disk, block mentor self-booking & time-slot overlaps, and move `DemoUpiProvider` onto `ClockService`.
8. **Admin Support & Abandoned-Project Closure Ticket Queue UI (`ADM-03`)** — Build `/admin/support` (`AdminSupport.jsx`) so platform administrators can view and resolve Phase 12 `Project Closure Request` tickets and Help & Support inquiries natively.
9. **Category Starter-Task Templates & Task Dependencies (`PRJ-09`)** — Provide 1-click starter task templates in `CreateProject.jsx` and optional `blockedByTaskId` dependency locks in `OwnedWorkspace.jsx` & `TaskCard.jsx`.
10. **Mentor Availability Calendar, Storage Hygiene Job & Modal Accessibility (`MEN-07`, `MOD-05`)** — Add recurring weekly UTC/local mentor availability slots in `MyMentorProfile.jsx`, add Job 19 `storageHygieneJob` to purge expired export files & orphan uploads on disk, and standardize modal ARIA/focus handling.
